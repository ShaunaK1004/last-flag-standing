Expressions

Eyes only = blinking, glancing eyes and eyebrows, no mouth. Full adds the mouth and every reaction: alert near an open hole, terrified in the final seconds, proud with a shield, dizzy on tumble boost, crowned on a win.

Skin

Classic = one hat per country. Cultural = a fuller look (headwear, drapes, jewellery, props) for countries with one defined, falling back to a classic hat otherwise.

Motion

Lively adds blinking, glancing, breathing squash-and-stretch and floating event icons on top of whatever expression/skin is on.
